How it works

No schedule to fall behind on.

1

Pick a weekly goal

Four sessions is the default. Sessions roll 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1, each one a focus area like Upper body or Prehab.

2

Train when it suits you

Open the app at the gym. It knows where you are, what equipment is there and how long you've got.

3

Rate, and it adapts

Too light, just right or too heavy. Next time's weight, reps and sets follow from what you tell it.

Features

Built for real gym sessions.

Weekly counter

A simple 2/4 that resets on Monday. Extra sessions count too, and nothing nags you about missed days.

Progress that follows you

Per-exercise feedback on effort, weight and volume sets the next target automatically.

Fits your time

Set 60 minutes and the session fits it. Long warm-up? Rest gets trimmed first, and you can always undo.

Knows your gym

Map each gym's equipment once. GPS picks the gym, and you'll never get an exercise it can't do.

One-tap swaps

Rack taken? Get the three best alternatives for the same muscles, available right here.

Works offline

Basement gym with no signal? Everything works and syncs when you're back online.

In the gym

Only what you need, when you need it.

  • During a setWeight, reps and one big Done button.
  • RestingA countdown that starts on its own, plus what's next.
  • After the sessionYour week, your time and next session's targets.
